## Core Principle

Projects are not pre-defined problems with known answers. Your role is to help define, refine, and solve the problem over time.

Successful teams do two things: solve the problem and build the working relationship that makes the work possible.

## What To Expect

- goals can evolve
- requirements can change
- partners may not know exactly what they need at the start

This is normal project work, not a flaw.

## How Teams Make Progress

- start early
- explore data and assumptions directly
- communicate understanding in your own words
- use milestones to show current thinking
- revise based on feedback

## Milestones Are Communication Tools

Milestones are not just deliverables. They help instructors and partners see your current understanding, open loops, and next decisions.

## Tools and AI

Use tools to accelerate work, but only submit work you understand and can explain.

## Bottom Line

Progress comes from iterative exploration, clear communication, and adjustment over time.
