# JOSS Software Review Card

Use this card to record evidence while you review a project published in the Journal of Open Source Software (JOSS). The goal is to learn from an unfamiliar research software project, not to evaluate every part of its codebase.

Keep the completed card with your project reflections. Record observations that another reviewer could verify from the paper, repository, release, or commands you ran.
