# Final Project Code Review - Day 2

Today completes the two-day deep review. Stay with the same pair or triad, switch roles, and give the second author the same sustained review attention received on Day 1.

## Agenda (80 minutes)

1. 5 min - Check-in and confirm Day 2 author and reviewer roles
2. 10 min - Review the Day 1 evidence and establish the Day 2 review plan
3. 50 min - Deep review blocks: author walkthrough, independent checks, and evidence-based feedback
4. 10 min - Author response, prioritized fix plan, and review-results exchange
5. 5 min - Share outcomes and complete the two-day review cycle

### Learning Goals

By the end of this class, you should be able to:

- complete a sustained review of a teammate's release-candidate project from the perspective of a future collaborator; and
- use specific review evidence to prepare a credible release candidate and handoff package.

## Group Peer Review

Stay with the Day 1 review group. Each reviewer starts from a fresh clone, follows only the README, and completes the [Quick Peer Review Form](../Guide/21-Peer-Review-Quick-Form.md).

- **Pairs:** Reverse the Day 1 roles. The Day 1 reviewer is now the author; the Day 1 author independently reviews the second project.
- **Triads:** Continue the timed rotation until every student has presented a project and served as both execution reviewer and documentation reviewer. Use any remaining time to recheck the most consequential blocker identified during Day 1.

Review focus:

- Can a reviewer reproduce the main workflow and identify the expected result?
- Are setup steps, dependencies, data access, and resource requirements stated accurately?
- Do tests, validation checks, and known limitations support the claims in the project documentation?
- Is the repository safe to share, without credentials or unreviewable private data?
- Does the author have a clear, evidence-based plan for addressing the highest-priority issue?

## Fix Your Own Repository

Exchange completed forms and make a short, prioritized fix plan. Begin one high-priority fix if time permits; complete the remaining fixes before the release-candidate deadline. Commit each completed fix and update the handoff note so it reflects the current project state.

## Before Next Class

- Push your release-candidate fixes and completed review form.
- Complete the [Release Candidate and Handoff Package](../22-next-sun-release-candidate-milestone.md) before its deadline.
- Keep a short list of any remaining final-project blockers. Day 26 will use newly formed review groups and is a separate final-review activity.
