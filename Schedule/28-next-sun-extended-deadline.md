# Extended Deadline

All students may submit their final project through {{ page.date | date: "%B %d, %Y" }}. You do not need to contact the instructor to use this extended deadline.

Your repository should include the required project materials, documentation, and presentation or demonstration materials described in the [Final Project Submission and Presentation Materials](./25-next-sun-final-project-submission-milestone.md).
