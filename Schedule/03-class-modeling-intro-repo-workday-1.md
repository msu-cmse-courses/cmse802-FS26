# Modeling Intro Project Workday 1

## Agenda (80 minutes)

1. 10 min - Check-in and goals for the day
2. 15 min - Quick walkthrough of repository structure and expectations
3. 45 min - In-class project work in the modeling_intro repository
4. 10 min - Progress share and blockers

### Learning Goals

By the end of this class, you should be able to:

- Navigate the modeling_intro repository workflow.
- Make measurable progress on your assigned project tasks.
- Document at least one decision, issue, or next step in your repository.

## In-Class Focus

Most of class time is dedicated to hands-on work in the shared project repository:

- Repository: <https://github.com/colbrydi/modeling_intro>

Work with your team to complete the current tasks, improve clarity in your project artifacts, and capture evidence of progress.

## Before Next Class

- Push your latest updates to the repository.
- Leave a short note in your repository (issue, README note, or commit message) describing your next task.
- Bring one blocker or open question for check-in.
